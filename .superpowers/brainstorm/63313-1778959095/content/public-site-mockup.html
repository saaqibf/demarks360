Questions
FAQ
How far in advance do I need to book?
We require a minimum of 7 days notice. The sooner you book, the better — popular dates fill up fast, especially weekends and holidays.
Is delivery and setup included?
Yes. Setup, teardown, and an on-site attendant for the full duration are included in every package at no extra charge. We serve all of Calgary.
How does payment work?
You pay a 30% deposit to secure your date. Your card is saved on file and the remaining 70% balance is automatically charged 7 days before your event. You'll receive a receipt for both.
Can I book if I'm outside Calgary?
We currently serve Calgary and the surrounding area only. Events must have an Alberta postal code beginning with "T".
