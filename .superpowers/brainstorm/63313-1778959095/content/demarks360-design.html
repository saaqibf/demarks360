Questions

Frequently
Asked

How far in advance do I need to book?
A minimum of 7 days notice is required. The sooner you book, the better — popular dates fill quickly, especially Friday and Saturday evenings.
Is delivery and setup included?
Yes — delivery, setup, teardown & a dedicated on-site attendant for the full duration are included in every package, anywhere in Calgary at no extra charge.
How does payment work?
A 30% deposit secures your date at booking. Your card is saved — with your explicit consent — and the remaining 70% balance is automatically charged 7 days before your event. A receipt is sent for both charges.
Can I see which time slots are already booked?
Yes. Our booking calendar shows the full day timeline — you can see exactly which time slots are taken and which are open, so two events can happen on the same day at different times.
Are there firecracker restrictions?
Cold spark firecrackers are a stunning add-on — however, they require venue approval. We recommend confirming with your venue before adding this option.
Do you serve areas outside Calgary?
We currently serve Calgary and the immediate surrounding area only. Events must have an Alberta postal code beginning with "T".
