# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Calgary-area residents and event planners organizing milestone celebrations: birthday parties (especially 21st, 30th, 50th), wedding receptions, corporate team events, graduations, family gatherings, and community celebrations. They range from 20-something friends planning a surprise to a mother organizing her daughter's wedding. What they share: they want their event to feel special and remembered. They are looking for a vendor they can trust, book easily, and forget about on the day itself.

Secondary users: corporate HR and event coordinators booking for company parties and brand activations.

Not served: nightclubs, bars, adult entertainment, or anything vulgar or inappropriate.

## Product Purpose

Demarks 360 is a Calgary-based 360-degree slow-motion video booth rental service, owned and operated by a Calgary Muslim mother. The business exists to make every event feel cinematic and shareable, with same-day gallery delivery so guests leave with something real. Success looks like: a visitor lands on the site, immediately understands what the booth does and how much it costs, feels warmly invited to book, and completes a booking with confidence. Post-event, happy clients should feel compelled to leave a review.

## Brand Personality

Bold, warm, welcoming. The brand is a proud Calgary local business with genuine heart. It celebrates every kind of family, every milestone, every community. It is confident without being flashy, professional without being corporate, and celebratory without being nightclub-adjacent. Think of a talented friend who happens to run the best photo booth in the city and genuinely wants your event to go well.

Three-word summary: **joyful, trustworthy, cinematic**

## Anti-references

- Nightclub/party supply sites: neon on black, Vegas energy, aggressive typography, anything that reads as adult entertainment
- Corporate event rental catalogs: dry, purely functional, price-list feeling, zero warmth
- Generic AI-built SaaS landing pages: purple gradients, floating cards, "Join 10,000+ users" social proof banners, sterile hero sections
- Cold over-designed luxury: too editorial, too fashion-magazine, feels untouchable or unapproachable, hard to actually book
- AI slop: anything that looks generated, templated, or generic. Every design decision must feel intentional and specific to this brand.

## Design Principles

1. **Welcome first.** Every person who lands on the site should feel like this business is for them. No one should feel excluded. Warmth is a design choice, not a personality trait.
2. **Show the magic, then explain it.** The 360 booth experience is hard to describe in words. Lead with the feeling (video, visual, motion) before the logistics.
3. **Make booking feel obvious.** The path from "I want this" to "I booked it" should have no friction. Pricing, availability, and the CTA must be clear at every scroll depth.
4. **Earn the review.** The post-event experience (gallery delivery, quality of video) should feel so good that leaving a review is the natural next step. The site sets that expectation.
5. **Local and real.** Calgary-specific details (service area, YYC references) are a feature, not a footnote. Being local is a trust signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. The service is intended to be welcoming to all Calgary communities. All interactive elements must meet touch-target minimums (44px). Color contrast must pass AA for all body text. Reduced-motion preference should be respected (already implemented via `.reveal` media query).
